# 🤖 AI Job & Skill Market Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The AI Job & Skill Market Intelligence Dashboard is a data analytics and business intelligence project designed to analyze how Artificial Intelligence is influencing the job market.

The project analyzes job demand, salary trends, AI exposure, automation risk, experience levels, employment types, remote work opportunities, and in-demand skills across different locations and industries.

The dashboard transforms job-market data into interactive visual insights to help understand changing employment patterns and the skills that are becoming increasingly relevant in an AI-driven workforce.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze AI-related job demand across industries and locations
- Identify high-demand job roles and skills
- Analyze salary trends across different roles and experience levels
- Understand the relationship between AI exposure and automation risk
- Compare employment and remote-work opportunities
- Analyze how job requirements vary by experience and education
- Identify skills that are increasingly important in the evolving job market
- Generate data-driven insights about future career opportunities

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis highlights how the job market is evolving with increasing AI adoption.

Some key observations include:

- AI-related skills are becoming increasingly relevant across multiple job roles and industries.
- Job demand and salary vary significantly based on role, experience, location, and required skills.
- Some roles show higher levels of AI exposure and automation risk than others.
- Remote-work opportunities differ across industries and employment types.
- Technical and AI-related skills can provide broader opportunities across the evolving job market.
- Continuous skill development is becoming increasingly important as job requirements change.

## 💡 Business & Career Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Professionals should continuously develop relevant technical and analytical skills.
- Organizations should monitor changing skill requirements when hiring and workforce planning.
- Job seekers should focus on skills that appear frequently across high-demand roles.
- Companies can use job-market trends to identify emerging skill gaps.
- Employees should adapt to AI-driven changes rather than relying only on traditional qualifications.
- Career planning should consider both current demand and changing technology requirements.
